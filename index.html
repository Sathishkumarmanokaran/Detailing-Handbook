Canadian Standards (CSA S16) - Standard Slot Dimensions

Slotted holes are permitted in bolted joints in accordance with CSA S16. Short slots may be used without regard to direction of loading in bearing-type connections, while long slots require careful consideration of load direction and washer requirements.
